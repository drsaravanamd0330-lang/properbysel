# Legal and compliance requirements matrix

**This is not legal advice.** It is an engineering checklist of laws and platform rules that likely apply to an Indian property listing marketplace, mapped to what the product does today. Every row needs confirmation by a qualified Indian lawyer (and a chartered accountant for tax) before launch. Where we were unsure, the row says so.

Status key: **Built** (in the product) · **Partly built** · **Not built** · **Decision needed** (business or lawyer)

| Area | Likely requirement | Where it applies in ProperBySel | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data protection: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules | Notice and consent in clear language; collect only what is needed; let users access, correct and erase data; reasonable security safeguards; breach notification; grievance contact | Login consent checkbox; minimal fields (phone, name, optional email); no Aadhaar collected; account deletion endpoint and buttons on web and mobile; draft privacy policy | **Partly built.** Privacy policy is a draft. Data export on request, consent records versioning, retention schedule and breach procedure need lawyer input. The Rules phase in obligations over time; confirm which dates apply. |
| Intermediary obligations: IT Act, 2000 and IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 | Publish terms and privacy policy; appoint a grievance officer and publish contact details; acknowledge complaints quickly and resolve within set timelines; act on unlawful content | Draft grievance page; report button on every listing; moderation queue and takedown | **Partly built.** Grievance officer name and contact are placeholders. Confirm timelines and whether "significant" thresholds could apply later. |
| Cyber incident reporting: CERT-In directions (2022) | Report specified cyber incidents within a short deadline; keep logs for a set period | API logs; audit log | **Not built** as a process. Log retention period and reporting contact need to be set. |
| Real estate regulation: RERA, 2016 and Tamil Nadu RERA rules | Registered projects must show their RERA registration number in advertisements; real estate agents generally must register with the state authority | Builder listings and agent accounts | **Not built.** Add a RERA number field for project and agent listings, show it publicly, and decide whether to require it before approval. Decision needed on how far the platform must check it. |
| Brokerage disclosure | Agents should disclose brokerage terms | `brokerageDisclosure` field on listings | **Partly built.** Field exists; make it mandatory for agent listings? Decision needed. |
| SMS and calls: TRAI TCCCPR, 2018 (DLT) | Register the business entity, sender ID and every SMS template on a DLT platform; no promotional messages to DND numbers without consent | OTP SMS via MSG91 | **Not built** (account needed). Only transactional OTP templates are planned. |
| Payments | Use an RBI-authorised payment aggregator; do not store card data; do not hold customer funds | Razorpay adapter; sandbox in development; no card data touches our servers; no deposits, escrow or property money collected | **Built** for the design. Razorpay merchant onboarding needed. |
| GST | Register when turnover crosses the threshold (or earlier if required for online services); issue tax invoices with GSTIN; charge the correct rate | 18% GST assumed on advertising services; receipts say "not a tax invoice" until `BUSINESS_GSTIN` is set; receipt numbering per financial year | **Partly built.** CA to confirm rate, place of supply, invoice format and whether registration is needed from day one. |
| Consumer protection: Consumer Protection Act, 2019, E-Commerce Rules, 2020, and CCPA guidelines on dark patterns (2023) | Clear pricing including taxes before payment; refund and cancellation policy; grievance redressal; no misleading claims or dark patterns | Pricing page shows GST-inclusive totals; draft refund policy; "Sponsored" label on paid placements; no fake urgency or fake reviews | **Partly built.** Confirm whether the E-Commerce Rules apply to a services listing platform and finalise the refund policy. |
| Advertising standards: ASCI Code | Paid placements clearly labelled; no misleading claims | "Sponsored" badge; verification badges only after a recorded check | **Built.** Review marketing copy before campaigns. |
| Aadhaar Act, 2016 | Do not collect or store Aadhaar unless legally permitted | Not collected; identity review uses other documents if ever needed | **Built** (by omission). Keep it that way. |
| Property documents | Handle sale deeds and tax receipts as sensitive; do not claim title is verified | Private documents encrypted at rest when stored in S3 (server-side encryption), signed short links, moderator access logged; footer says ProperBySel does not verify legal title | **Built.** Lawyer to review disclaimer wording. |
| Children | Service is for adults; avoid processing children's data | Terms draft says users must be 18+ | **Partly built.** No age gate beyond terms. |
| Accessibility | Good practice and possible procurement requirements (WCAG 2.1 AA) | Semantic HTML, labels, keyboard navigation, contrast tokens | **Partly built.** No formal audit yet. |
| Google Play policies | Privacy policy URL, Data safety form, in-app account deletion, accurate permissions | Account deletion built; camera/photos permission requested only when adding photos | **Partly built.** Data safety form and store listing not prepared; needs a Play developer account. |
| Apple App Store guidelines | Privacy nutrition labels, in-app account deletion, sign-in rules, accurate metadata | Account deletion built | **Partly built.** Needs an Apple Developer account and App Privacy answers. |
| Map data | Respect OpenStreetMap tile usage policy; production volume needs a commercial tile provider | Leaflet with OSM tiles | **Decision needed** before launch traffic. |
| Intellectual property | Do not copy competitor content; own brand assets | Original brand mark and copy; demo photos are generated placeholders | **Built.** Trademark search and filing for "ProperBySel": decision needed. |

## Documents to finalise with a lawyer

Privacy policy, terms of use, refund and cancellation policy, grievance redressal page (with named officer), listing and content policy, advertiser agreement (for agents and builders), cookie notice if analytics are added.

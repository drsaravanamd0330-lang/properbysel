## Copy

- **App name**: ProperBySel – Rent, Buy, Sell (Play limit 30 characters; App Store 30)
- **Subtitle (App Store, 30)**: Homes, land & rentals in India
- **Short description (Play, 80)**: Find homes, land and rentals in Chennai. Post your property free.
- **Keywords (App Store, 100)**: property,rent,house,flat,apartment,land,plot,chennai,real estate,buy home,sell,tenant,owner,pg

**Full description**

ProperBySel is India's Smart Property Marketplace for renting, buying and selling land, homes and commercial spaces. We are starting in Chennai.

For people looking:
• Search by locality, budget, bedrooms, furnishing and property type
• See listings on a map (locations are approximate until you talk to the owner)
• Save properties and searches, and get notified about new matches
• Send an enquiry and chat with owners, agents and builders in the app
• Request and manage site visits

For owners, agents and builders:
• Post a property free in a few minutes, with photos
• Every listing is reviewed by our team before it goes live
• Manage enquiries, conversations and visits in one inbox
• Choose whether to share your phone number; it is never shown publicly
• Optional featured placement to reach more people

Safety first: never pay a token amount before visiting a property and checking documents. Report any suspicious listing from its page. ProperBySel does not collect property payments or deposits and does not verify legal title.

A product of AAROVIN SOFTWARE SOLUTIONS.

Do not add customer numbers, ratings, awards or "verified" claims until they are true and evidenced.

## Store forms to prepare

- Privacy policy URL (final version after legal review)
- Google Play Data safety: phone number, name, email (optional), approximate and precise location (only if the user enters an address), photos, messages; data encrypted in transit; deletion available in app
- App Store App Privacy: same data types, linked to the user, not used for tracking
- Content rating questionnaire; target audience 18+
- Account deletion: in app under Account → Delete my account (both stores require this)
- Demo login for app review: a dedicated reviewer phone number with a fixed test OTP is **not** built; Apple and Google reviewers need a working login, so plan a reviewer account on staging-like rules before submission
